Table~\ref{tab:notation_terminology} lists each such term together with a one-sentence definition and the section in which it is first introduced. Table~\ref{tab:notation_abbreviations} lists the abbreviations used throughout the thesis in alphabetical order.

Mobile Cluster & Virtual aggregate entity formed from the averaged centroids of the three individually-tracked mobile pallets, used to avoid redundant per-pallet waypoints and inter-robot collisions. & \autoref{sec:ch4_mobile_cluster} \\
Spatial Mutex (Corridor Claim Protocol) & Peer-to-peer, TTL-leased mutual exclusion scheme granting one robot at a time exclusive occupancy of a narrow aisle, without a central lock server. & \autoref{sec:ch5_spatial_mutex} \\
Asymmetric Race-Condition Resolution (LCFS Overwrite) & Practical substitute for the timestamp-and-identifier tie-breaking relation, whereby the most recently received claim packet unconditionally overwrites the local lock registry. & \autoref{sec:ch5_race_resolution} \\
Steel-Column Structural Bypass & Fixed-offset correction relocating Section~C's approach coordinate on two big-shelf faces to $Y = -13.50\,\text{m}$, clearing the inflation radius of a structural warehouse column. & \autoref{sec:ch5_steel_bypass} \\
Orientation-Driven (Dead-End Wall) Sweeping & Physics-informed reversal of a shelf's section-visiting order near the eastern and northern perimeter walls, preventing the robot from being driven into a corner at the end of a sweep. & \autoref{sec:ch5_deadend_sweep} \\
Hierarchical Two-Stage AMCL Recovery & Escalating localization-recovery ladder that attempts an in-place local spin before falling back to full global particle-filter reinitialization. & \autoref{sec:ch6_hierarchical_recovery} \\
Cyber-Physical Costmap Margin Paradox & The tension whereby relaxing the AMCL covariance gate to reduce nuisance recovery triggers simultaneously narrows the probabilistic safety margin against the local costmap's inflation boundary. & \autoref{sec:ch6_margin_paradox} \\
Pythagorean Parallel-Axis Coordinate Shifting & Reactive relocation of an inspection waypoint along the shelf-parallel axis, computed from the Pythagorean relation between a failed peer's occlusion radius and the perpendicular offset, to preserve line of sight around the stalled robot. & \autoref{sec:ch6_pythagorean_shift} \\
Two-Strike Blacklist Propagation & Fleet-wide broadcast permanently excluding a shelf side from the task pool once a single robot has failed to approach it twice. & \autoref{sec:ch6_two_strike} \\
Battery-Proportional Decentralized Workload Re-Allocation & One-shot redistribution of a failed robot's unfinished targets among survivors, split in proportion to each survivor's remaining state of charge. & \autoref{sec:ch6_battery_realloc} \\
Gate-Based Deterministic Launch Architecture & Readiness-polling launch sequencing (world, clock, topic, and lifecycle gates) chained via \texttt{OnProcessExit} handlers in place of fixed-duration timer delays. & \autoref{sec:ch7_gate_primitives} \\
